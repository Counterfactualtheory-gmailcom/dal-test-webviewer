COMPAiSS Research Library

Research & Publications

A consolidated library of COMPAiSS research on institutional AI governance, execution-gated generation, authorization-first architecture, hallucination risk, enterprise RAG, regulatory alignment, institutional deployment, empirical evaluation, and the economics of governed AI.

COMPAiSS research examines a central question for regulated institutions: how should artificial intelligence operate when the authority to provide an answer matters as much as the factual plausibility of the answer itself?

The materials below document the architectural, empirical, regulatory, operational, and economic case for governing AI before institutional inference occurs rather than attempting to control risk only after generation.

HTML research papers and analyses are available elsewhere on the COMPAiSS site. This library also provides direct, crawlable links to the supporting PDF research corpus and technical figures.
